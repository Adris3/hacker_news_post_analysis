# Hacker News Post Analysis

The first goal of this analysis is to determine if "Ask HN" or "Show HN" posts receive more comments than average. The second goal is to determine if the time at which a post is made determines how many comments it gets. 

See analysis.ipynb for full analysis.

# Conclusion

I analyzed posts on Hacker News to determine which categories of posts received the most comments on average.

Specifically, I first sorted the posts by whether they were ask posts, show posts, or general posts. After sorting, I calculated the average number of posts each category gets. It was clear ask posts got more engagement on average. 

Next, I sorted the average number of comments for each ask post by hour to determine which hour was likely to get the most engagement. 

Based on this analysis, one could say of the posts that received comments, ask post had more engagement on average, and tended to have the most engagement between the hours of 3:00PM EST and 4:00PM EST.
